        Definitions that change the answer

        • Net revenue is after discounts and returns. Using the pre-return figure overstates every margin by roughly 8 points.
        • Returns are attributed to the original order month, so margin answers “was this sale profitable”. The cash view is separate and will not tie.
        • Shipping is costed on billable weight — carriers bill max(actual, dimensional).
        • Cost of goods is net of what was recovered from resale, and returns carry their own freight and restocking cost. Nothing is allocated by rule of thumb.

        What is deliberately absent

        No contribution margin after advertising by state or category. Ad platforms do not report spend at those grains, so a geographic CM3 would be an allocation presented as a measurement.
